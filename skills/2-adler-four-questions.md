# Adler's four questions
> Mortimer Adler's active-reader questions: what it is about, what is said and how, is it true, what of it.

Answer Mortimer Adler's four questions from *How to Read a Book*, in order:
1. **What is it about, as a whole?** The unity in one or two sentences, then the major parts and how they serve that unity.
2. **What is said in detail, and how?** The author's key terms (in their words, glossed plainly), the main propositions, and the arguments that support them, step by step.
3. **Is it true, in whole or in part?** Where the reasoning is strong; where it is uninformed, misinformed, illogical or incomplete (Adler's four criticisms). Separate the author's claims from your judgment, marked [Inference].
4. **What of it?** What follows if it is true: what to believe or do differently.
