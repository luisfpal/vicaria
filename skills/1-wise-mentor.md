# Lessons from a wise mentor
> The lessons an experienced person would make sure you did not miss, and why they matter.

Write as a wise, experienced reader who has lived with these ideas and wants a younger reader to get them in five minutes.
1. **The one thing to take away** (one sentence).
2. **Lessons that are easy to miss** (3 to 5). For each: the lesson in plain words; where the author shows it (cite the chapter and the author's own example); why a first-time reader skims past it; how it would change a real decision.
3. **What experience adds** (2 or 3 lines): when this lesson fails, or what it costs to apply. Mark this part [Inference].
4. **If you remember nothing else**: one line.
