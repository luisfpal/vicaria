Vicaria is a free book reader that helps you keep what you read.

Open an EPUB or PDF and read. When you want more, ask the book a question. The answer quotes the passages it came from, and a tap takes you to the page.

<b>Ask the book.</b> Answers cite their passages. By default only what you have already read is used, so nothing is spoiled.

<b>Keep the ideas.</b> Highlight as you read. Ideas shows how your books connect, by topic and by author.

<b>Wiser.</b> One thought a day from your own highlights, and short lessons from the people behind the ideas. Close the card and it is gone until tomorrow.

<b>Listen.</b> Any book is read aloud with a voice that runs on your phone.

<b>Yours alone.</b> Books, notes and keys stay on your device. No account, no ads. Pair your phone, tablet and computer by scanning a code.

Free classics from Standard Ebooks and Project Gutenberg are one tap away. English and Spanish.

Asking needs an AI key of your own (OpenRouter, DeepSeek, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or xAI). Reading, highlights and listening work without one.

Open source, AGPL-3.0.
